# Architecture

Equity Lake is a local-first, multi-market equity EOD data platform: ingestion,
a numbered medallion Delta lake (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum), Hamilton-driven
feature engineering, ML inference, and signals — operated through the unified
`equity` CLI.

The canonical architecture document now lives at
[docs/developer/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/developer/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md)
(moved here on 2026-08-29). It is also published in the MkDocs site under the
Developer → Architecture section.
