## 3. Price basis — the probe falsified "explicitly unadjusted"

`adj_close` differs from `close` on all 1,260 rows, and the two columns behave differently:

| Ticker | `adj_close/close` range | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AAPL | 0.987205 – 0.992042 | dividend back-adjustment in `adj_close` only |
| MSFT | 0.979097 – 0.986419 | same |
| NVDA | 0.997110 – 0.997393 | dividend only — **no split factor in `adj_close`** |
| JPM | 0.938358 – 0.960766 | same |
| KO | 0.924596 – 0.952835 | same |

NVDA's 10-for-1 split (ex 2024-06-10, lake-convention action value `0.1`) is the decisive
evidence:

| | 2024-06-07 (last pre-split) | 2024-06-10 (ex date) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `close` | 120.888 | 121.790 | **+0.746 %** |
| `volume` | 412,386,000 | 313,434,100 | 0.76× |

A truly unadjusted series would show a 10× close drop and a 10× volume jump. It shows
neither: **OHLC and volume are split-back-adjusted by the vendor**; only `adj_close`
carries dividend adjustment on top. The slice is "vendor split-adjusted,
dividend-unadjusted", not raw.

`actions.parquet` sha256 `2799e99870b4ddc0668b4166dcf06f61e3449445b8f971b0d51e7fa8f9aed8d0`.

`since=2024-01-01` is an exclusive lower bound, so the call returned every later event
(2024: 21, 2025: 20, 2026: 17) — the action window is **not** bounded by the price window.
A landing must pass explicit watermarks and surface both edges.

The null clock is the D00 finding on real rows: there is no source-issued availability
time, so no strict point-in-time adjustment and no Gold feature write that requires a
coverage proof can be supported by this data as-is.

## 5. What this probe proves and does not prove

Proves: the provider is reachable from this environment; the adapter's identity, retry and
digest paths work on real rows; 2024 coverage for the sample is complete against the
repository's calendar; actions are retrievable including splits in the lake convention;
the throttle seam is usable at 20 rpm for USD 0.

Does not prove: adjusted or point-in-time correctness (no source clock, no action proof, no
vintage detector); action completeness or the absence of corrections below the watermark;
survivorship or historical universe membership (the configured list is current); that the
provider will keep serving these endpoints; or licensing suitability beyond the owner's
local-only position.

## 6. The blocking finding

Ingesting these rows as they arrive would break the adjusted-read contract. `Close` is
already split-adjusted, while `with_price_adjustment` (ADR-0015) assumes unadjusted input
and multiplies by the cumulative split ratio — nine call sites in features, labels,
tracking and backtesting. For NVDA that is a second 10× adjustment on every pre-split row.
The only existing guard refuses frames already stamped `price_adjustment_version`, which
Bronze price rows are not.

Consequently no real price row may enter `data/lake/` until one of two shapes is accepted
in the P00-2 ADR: re-inflate to raw prices at ingest (keeps the current contract), or store
the vendor basis and stamp it so adjusted consumers fail closed (a Bronze schema change).
The demo lake compounds this: its `corporate_action_coverage` proof is
`source=synthetic_demo_seed`, `coverage_status=complete`, valid through 2026-10-28, and
would pass validation for real rows unless the lake is reset or the real slice is given a
distinct path.
