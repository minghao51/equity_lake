## Planted and null controls (complete sets, nothing censored)

| Set | Seeds requested | Seeds run | Decision rule fired | One-sided 95% binomial bound | Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Null, unrelated-noise labels | 100 (`1729…1828`) | 100 | 0 false positives | upper **0.029513** | ≤ 0.10 ✔ |

- Per-seed cost measured on this host: null-noise median `0.0160 s`, null-permuted median
  `0.0263 s`, planted median `0.0286 s`; the whole controls block took `7.11 s` of the `7200 s`
  aggregate ceiling (0.1%). Nothing is censored, so the power claim is admissible.
- **Individual null seeds were never required to lose** and none happened to fire; the frozen
  criteria are the two bounds above, not a per-seed requirement.
- The four planted seeds that did not fire are `11755, 11758, 11760, 11769` — reported, not removed.
  Planted mean per-session net return across seeds: `+0.00114` to `+0.00903`; null range:
  `-0.00380` to `+0.00163`. The cost drag (`58.5 bp` per five-session block, `≈11.7 bp/session`)
  is why the null loses without any signal being planted.
- The power lower bound clears by 2.6 percentage points. That is a real, narrow pass at the frozen
  50-seed size: it is the measured number, not a margin to be widened after the fact.

## Scale points (5 / 50 / 200 instruments, three-year design)

Three-year design `2022-01-03 … 2024-12-31` = 782 sessions; warmup 25 sessions excluded; minimum
universe 5 instruments per decision session; 450 decision sessions per point.

| Size | Wall time | Peak RSS | RSS ceiling | I/O (read/write bytes) | Feature rows | Long decisions | Technical long | Min universe seen | Parity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | `0.030 s` | `320.1 MB` | `8192 MB` | `0 / 0` (available) | 3,785 | 914 | 1,136 | 5 | reference |
| 50 | `0.077 s` | `367.1 MB` | `8192 MB` | `0 / 0` (available) | 37,832 | 15,504 | 11,369 | 49 | features ✔, technical decisions ✔ |

- **Ceilings**: per size `0.030 / 0.077 / 0.221 s` against `1800 s`; aggregate `5.39 s` against
  `7200 s`; peak RSS `484.9 MB` against `8192 MB`. Nothing censored, nothing widened.
- **Output parity**: the five-instrument fixture is byte-identical inside the 50- and 200-instrument
  batches (feature digest and model-free technical-decision digest equal), and at size 5 the
  vectorised economics equals R02's per-session reference path exactly
  (`absolute_difference = 0.0`). *Model-based* decisions are not required to agree across sizes —
  the pooled fit differs by construction, and the artefact says so.
- **I/O**: `/proc/self/io` is available on this host, so the counters are measured values
  (`read_bytes` and `write_bytes` are genuinely `0` for a fixture generated in memory); an
  unavailable counter would be reported `available: false` **with a reason**, never as zero — the
  test `test_unavailable_io_counters_carry_a_reason_and_never_zeros` pins that.
- **Fixture provenance and health**: no shipped pool holds 200 instruments (`us_equity` 44 +
  `cn_ashare` 36 + `hk_sg_equity` 25, de-duplicated to 104), so the scale fixtures are
  `synthetic_ohlcv` + `MARKET_CONFIGS` profiles: shipped pools in order, then deterministic
  `SCALE####` ids. The recorded diagnostics show `degenerate_paths = 0` and a maximum single-session
  move of `11.1% / 13.3% / 14.1%`, i.e. no path is sitting on the generator's price floor. The
  per-session return at a scale point is recorded for completeness and is **not** economic evidence.
- **Universe duplication found and fixed before the run**: `TEST_DATA_TICKERS["cn_ashare"]` lists
  `601988` twice; the scale pool is de-duplicated so a size is a count of distinct instruments
  (`test_the_scale_universe_is_distinct_instruments_and_reaches_two_hundred`).

## Honest limitations

1. **Synthetic fixtures only.** Every control runs on `synthetic_ohlcv` bars; no lake, no real root,
   no network. The six leakage controls exercise the *production* seams
   (`filter_as_of_available`, `PurgedEmbargoedWalkForwardSplitter`, `validate_feature_action_coverage`,
   `validate_action_coverage_for_decision`) plus one harness path (the append-after-cutoff pipeline),
   so a passing control is evidence about the code, not about data quality.
2. **One seeding cost.** During harness development, at the superseded `0.4%/session` plant strength,
   predeclared planted seeds `11729` and `11730` were inspected before the freeze. Both are recorded
   in the preregistration and remain in the frozen set; the frozen strength came from a design
   calculation on seeds `1001–1005`, which are outside both predeclared sets.
3. **The planted control is deliberately large** (`+1.0%/session` on positive-momentum sessions) so a
   compliant harness must detect it. It measures detection power, not a market effect.
4. **Not run here / not claimed:** the full unit suite, any refit or retune of R02, any promotion,
   any point-in-time claim, any economic or capacity claim from the scale numbers, and any holdout
   contact (the control fixture's last 20 sessions are untouched by the run).
