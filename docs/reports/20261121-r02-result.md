**Estimand clarification for the paired-difference line above.** The two numbers in
"Paired difference (primary − technical) net return: −2.79 pp, 95% block-bootstrap interval [−0.42 pp, +0.31 pp]"
are not the same estimand, which is why the point estimate sits outside the interval. Recomputed offline from
`predictions.parquet` and the frozen Gold closes with the frozen estimators
(`ml/experiment.py`, block 5 sessions, 2000 resamples, seed 20261121):

| Reading | Estimand | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| −2.79 pp | difference of **compounded** net returns for each arm (`cumprod(1+r)` minus one) | −0.0279030349 (−2.79 pp) — no interval was recorded for this quantity |
| −0.04 pp per session | **mean per-session** net-return difference on matched dates | −0.0003740027 (−0.0374 pp per session) |
| [−0.42 pp, +0.31 pp] | 95% interval **for that mean per-session difference** (`_bootstrap_paired_difference` resamples `mean` of per-session differences) | [−0.0041605634, +0.0030657660] |

The interval brackets −0.000374 and cannot bracket −0.0279: it resamples a per-session mean, not a compounded
difference. So the sentence above pairs a compounded point estimate with a per-session interval — a **documentation
and aggregation mismatch, not a computation error**. The interval is a correct 95% interval for the mean per-session
difference, which includes zero; the compounded difference of −2.79 pp has no interval in this run.

**Nothing else changes.** Every other number above was recomputed and reproduced exactly (Brier, ROC AUC, net
return, Sharpe and max drawdown for all four arms; the validation fold; all 42 bootstrap intervals — 16
discrimination, 24 economic, 2 paired; the comparison deltas; the promotion verdict at 1 of 5 criteria, `risk`
only). The `economics` criterion fails on its first condition (the compounded difference is negative), so the verdict
is unaffected by the interval reading. `promoted` remains **false**, there is no refit, no retune, no holdout read,
and no promotion.
